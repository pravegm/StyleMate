# Fashionpedia dataset
licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (annotations)
kind: Training data (garment categories the labeler was trained on)
source: https://fashionpedia.github.io/home/data_license.html

The garment labeler ("SegFormer garment labeler (Fashionpedia)") was trained on data derived from **Fashionpedia** by Menglin Jia, Mengyun Shi, Mikhail Sirotenko, Yin Cui, Claire Cardie, Bharath Hariharan, Hartwig Adam and Serge Belongie (https://fashionpedia.github.io/home/). No Fashionpedia images or annotations are included in StyleMate; the model learned from them.

The Fashionpedia annotations are licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The images are not owned by Fashionpedia; their use follows the terms of the sites they came from.

Citation: M. Jia, M. Shi, M. Sirotenko, Y. Cui, C. Cardie, B. Hariharan, H. Adam, S. Belongie, "Fashionpedia: Ontology, Segmentation, and an Attribute Localization Dataset", ECCV 2020.

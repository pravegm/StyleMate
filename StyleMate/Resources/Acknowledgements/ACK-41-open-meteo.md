When MET Norway can't be reached, weather data come from **Open-Meteo.com** (https://open-meteo.com), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Open-Meteo builds on open data from national weather services, listed at https://open-meteo.com/en/licence.

StyleMate turns the forecast into a short description and a symbol, and uses it to suggest outfits. Open-Meteo does not endorse StyleMate.

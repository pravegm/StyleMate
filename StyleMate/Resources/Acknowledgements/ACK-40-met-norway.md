Weather forecasts are **based on data from MET Norway** (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute), https://api.met.no. MET Norway's data are licensed under the Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data (NLOD) 2.0 (https://data.norge.no/nlod/en/2.0) and the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

StyleMate turns the forecast into a short description and a symbol, and uses it to suggest outfits. Only your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) is sent, to ask for the forecast. MET Norway does not endorse StyleMate.

StyleMate Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · applies to the StyleMate iPhone app

In short
  • You sign in with Sign in with Apple. StyleMate gets only a private ID from Apple and, if you share it, your name. There is no StyleMate server: we don't collect, receive or sell any data about you.
  • Your photos, your selfie and the face data made from them are analysed only on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • Your wardrobe syncs only to your own iCloud (Apple's private database for your Apple ID), which we cannot read.
  • Delete your account and everything with it at any time: Profile → Account → Delete Account.

What StyleMate does

StyleMate looks through the photos you allow it to see, finds the ones of you, cuts out the clothes and accessories you're wearing and files them into a digital wardrobe. It then suggests outfits from those clothes. All of this runs on your iPhone using machine-learning models that ship inside the app.

Your account (Sign in with Apple)

StyleMate requires Sign in with Apple, because your wardrobe, your 50 free wardrobe pieces and your StyleMate Premium subscription belong to your account on every iPhone you use. StyleMate asks Apple for your name only (to greet you), and never for your email address.

At every launch StyleMate asks Apple whether your sign-in is still valid. If you stop using Apple sign-in for StyleMate, the app signs you out. If the Apple ID you sign in with is different from the one your iPhone uses for iCloud, your wardrobe is backed up in the iPhone's iCloud account, in a space that belongs only to your StyleMate account. Two people who share one iCloud never see each other's wardrobes.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, your wardrobe items (their images and descriptions) are stored in StyleMate's container in your own private iCloud database, so they survive a new phone. This is Apple's service, tied to your Apple ID; the developer has no access to it. Your photos, selfie and face data are never synced.

Services StyleMate talks to

  • Weather — to suit outfits to the weather, the app sends your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) to the free weather services MET Norway and Open-Meteo. Nothing else is sent, and only if you allow location access.
  • Apple — iCloud (above), and, on devices with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device language model to write short item details. StyleMate never uses Apple's cloud models.

StyleMate contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

StyleMate Premium (subscriptions)

StyleMate is free for your first 50 wardrobe items; StyleMate Premium (an auto-renewable subscription) makes the wardrobe unlimited. Purchases, renewals, refunds and cancellations are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. StyleMate never receives your payment details or your Apple ID. The app checks whether your subscription is active on the iPhone itself, using Apple's StoreKit; nothing about your purchase is sent to us. Manage or cancel in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you turn on the morning outfit, StyleMate schedules a local notification on your iPhone. The look in it is chosen on the device; no notification service or server is involved.

Sample photos

"Try with sample photos" uses a few public-domain (CC0) street-style photos bundled with the app. Sample clothes are labelled and can be removed in one tap.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your iPhone (and your iCloud, for wardrobe items) until you delete it. Profile → Account → Delete Account removes your wardrobe (including pieces waiting for Premium), outfit history, style learning, selfie, face data and scan history from the device, deletes your account's wardrobe from your iCloud, and removes the account from the device. Signing out keeps your data on the device for the next time you sign in. To also stop Apple sign-in for StyleMate, go to Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → StyleMate → Stop Using. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription: do that in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting the app removes everything on the device; you can also delete StyleMate's iCloud data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

StyleMate is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
